Walk around the truck you'll test in.

Every CDL skills test starts with a pre-trip inspection. Here's what you'll learn to check, and where.

Signature moment. On desktop the photo on the left stays put and changes as you scroll the checklist: 8 real photos of their training truck from 8 angles, one shoot. On phones each step shows its own photo, no scroll tricks. Replaces the AI-video idea. A 3D render can drop into the same slot later.
Frame 1 of 8. Front of the tractor, straight on, headlights on.
  1. Frame 1. Front of the tractor, straight on, headlights on.

    Front of the tractor

    Lights, reflectors and steering.

    • Headlights, clearance lights, turn signals
    • Steering box, linkage, no loose parts
    • Windshield and wipers
  2. Frame 2. Hood tilted open, engine bay from the driver's side.

    Engine compartment

    Fluids, belts and hoses.

    • Oil, coolant and power steering levels
    • Belt tension and wear
    • Hoses for leaks and cracks
  3. Frame 3. Low angle on the front steer tire and brake chamber.

    Steer axle

    Tires, wheels and brakes.

    • Tread depth and inflation
    • Lug nuts and hub oil seal
    • Brake chamber, hoses, slack adjuster
  4. Frame 4. Driver's side, fuel tank and steps, mid-body.

    Side of the tractor

    Fuel tank, mirrors and doors.

    • Fuel tank secure, cap tight, no leaks
    • Mirrors and brackets
    • Steps and hand holds
  5. Frame 5. Between tractor and trailer: fifth wheel, kingpin, air lines.

    Coupling

    Fifth wheel, kingpin, air and electric lines.

    • Locking jaws closed around the kingpin
    • No gap between fifth wheel and trailer apron
    • Glad hands sealed, lines not chafing
  6. Frame 6. Trailer side, landing gear and frame, wide.

    Trailer

    Landing gear, frame and tandems.

    • Landing gear raised, crank secured
    • Frame and cross members
    • Trailer tires, wheels and brakes
  7. Frame 7. Rear of the trailer at dusk, lights on.

    Rear of the trailer

    Lights, doors and mud flaps.

    • Brake lights, tail lights, signals
    • Doors and hinges latched
    • Reflective tape and flaps
  8. Frame 8. Inside the cab, over the student's shoulder, gauges lit.

    In the cab

    Air brake check.

    • Air pressure build-up and leak-down
    • Low-air warning and spring brakes
    • Gauges, horn, wipers, seat belt
Check with an instructor: inspection items above follow the standard CDL pre-trip; their instructors should confirm wording and order to match how they teach it.
